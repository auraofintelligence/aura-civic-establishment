168 hours in a week

Plan the place through time.

A 24/7 establishment lets people work with schedules that fit learning, care, paid work, creativity and other responsibilities.

Facility access and programme hours are separate decisions. An evening member might use a booked desk while a host runs a workshop elsewhere. A service provider may offer appointments only during its staffed hours. Maintenance has an allocated place in the calendar.

The time model therefore records opening hours, bookable availability, actual bookings and changeovers. Each shared asset keeps one identity even when several programmes use it.
