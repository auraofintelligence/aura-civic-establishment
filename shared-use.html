24/7 as a possibility

Whose hours could this place work for?

People combine learning, care, paid work, creativity and other commitments in different ways. A place available across the day and night could open up different possibilities.

The starting idea is 24/7 overlap and shared spaces with KYC. There is no settled daily timetable, room calendar or operating system for this building.

Which activities could happen alongside each other? Which could use the same room at different times? What would people actually want to use, and when?

Questions for development

What would sharing involve?

QuestionWhat could help explore it
What could overlap?The activities people are interested in and the resources they might share.
How might people use the space?Preferences about access, KYC, privacy, availability and assistance.
What happens between uses?Room setup, equipment, changeover time and the practical differences between activities.
Who might take part?People and organisations interested in exploring a role, with their own expectations and questions.
