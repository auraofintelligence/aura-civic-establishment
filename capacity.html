Planning people are an area allowance, not an approved occupancy. The model does not establish a present room layout. Member equivalents are weekly use at the stated average, before actual booking peaks are resolved.

Interpret the result

Ask what is driving the number.

ChangeWhat to examine
More opening hoursWhich hours people will use, and how access and support are provided.
More people per sessionThe actual activity, room, equipment and supervision.
Less turnoverWhether setup, cleaning and handover still fit.
More member equivalentsPeak demand, preferred booking times and repeat attendance.
More reserved support spaceReception, circulation, toilets, storage, staff facilities and technical services.
