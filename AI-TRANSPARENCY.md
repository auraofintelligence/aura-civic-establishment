# AI assistance and source status

This site develops Luke Nathan Hayes’s authored project material and the research supplied for the proposed civic establishment. AI tools assisted with source extraction, synthesis, website implementation, arithmetic checks and concept-image generation.

The new architectural concepts were generated with OpenAI image generation through Codex on 7 November 2026. Exact prompts, tool metadata and saved project assets are recorded in data/generated-image-provenance.json. Captions identify concepts individually. They do not depict the candidate property’s existing interior or establish a fit-out design.

Reused concept imagery credits its originating Aura project. Photographs identify real subjects and retain the creator’s licence and attribution. They are not generative replacements for documentary evidence of this property.

The whole system is still in development, including the Dunwich concept, where no programme is confirmed. Building evidence, authored proposals and scenario inputs have different statuses. Linked websites and repositories are published material, not confirmation of programmes or operating arrangements. Calculators expose their formulas and assumptions. Government portfolio mapping identifies responsibilities and potential subject alignment. It does not announce support, funding or a partnership.

AI-written schedules and access-policy outcomes were removed on 8 November 2026 following the author's correction: the site is an exploration, not a declaration of a process or system. The KYC explorer now offers questions rather than permissions or policy answers. The resource mapping is illustrative, and programme combinations start unselected. The site does not perform KYC, collect identity documents or grant building permissions. Scenario values and optional guide selections can be stored locally in a browser, exported as JSON or included in a user-chosen scenario link.

Discovery through llms.txt, a sitemap or public source access does not grant additional reuse or AI-training rights. See LICENCE.md and the separate licences for third-party photographs.
