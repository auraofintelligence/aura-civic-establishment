Interpretation and updates

Carry the correction forward.

The October installation plan preserves a federation of distinct organisations, finite investment arrangements, local ownership and ongoing renewal. Its current direction takes precedence over inconsistent archive wording.

The C-Hour planning edition’s correction register preserves community-chosen reciprocity while removing monetary conversion, wage substitution and financial-asset claims. The participant experience remains a proposal to develop through useful agent interactions and local pilots.

Building measurements and political responsibilities can change. Dates and links let a later review establish what needs updating. Original documents retain their history.
